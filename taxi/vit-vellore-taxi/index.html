Today’s fares

VIT main gate to Katpadi Junction is about 3 km by road.

Open the app to see today’s fare for your trip.

Fares are by distance; tolls and parking are extra. A night charge applies late at night. The exact fare for your pickup and drop is shown in the app before you book.

About this trip

VIT is in Katpadi, a short ride from the railway station. Autos follow the Government fare, and cars are priced by distance.

Going home for the holidays? Share one car to Chennai Airport or Bengaluru with friends; the outstation fare is for the whole vehicle, not per person.

How to book

  1. Open velloretaxi.app on your phone. Nothing to install.
  2. Choose your pickup and drop. The fare for every vehicle is shown before you book.
  3. Confirm with an OTP on your mobile number. You get the driver’s name and vehicle number when a driver accepts.

Questions

How much is an auto from VIT to Katpadi station?

It is about 3 km. Autos charge the Tamil Nadu Government fare: ₹50 for the first 1.8 km, then ₹25 per km.

Do you go from VIT to Chennai Airport?

Yes. See the Vellore to Chennai Airport page for today’s one-way fare for each vehicle.

Can I pay by UPI?

Yes. You can pay the driver by cash or UPI at the end of the trip.
