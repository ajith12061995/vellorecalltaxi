Taxi to and from CMC Hospital, Vellore

Patients and families visiting Christian Medical College Hospital can book an auto or a car to the hospital and back, with the fare shown first.

Today’s fares

Katpadi Junction to CMC Hospital (town campus) is about 7 km by road.

Open the app to see today’s fare for your trip.

Fares are by distance; tolls and parking are extra. A night charge applies late at night. The exact fare for your pickup and drop is shown in the app before you book.

About this trip

Many visitors to CMC come from other states and do not know local rates. The app shows the fare for each vehicle before you book, in English or Tamil.

For a day of appointments you can also take an hourly rental, where one vehicle and driver stay with you.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

How to book

  1. Open velloretaxi.app on your phone. Nothing to install.
  2. Choose your pickup and drop. The fare for every vehicle is shown before you book.
  3. Confirm with an OTP on your mobile number. You get the driver’s name and vehicle number when a driver accepts.

Questions

How do I get from Katpadi railway station to CMC?

Book an auto or a car in the app from Katpadi Junction to CMC Hospital. The trip is about 7 km.

Can someone else book for a patient?

Yes. When you book, choose “someone else” and enter the passenger’s name and number.

Can the driver wait while we are at the hospital?

For long visits use Rental in the app: the vehicle waits and you pay by the hours and km in the package.
