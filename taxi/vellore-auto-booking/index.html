About this trip

Autos on Vellore Taxi charge the Tamil Nadu Government revised fare: ₹50 for the first 1.8 km and ₹25 for every km after that. Between 10 pm and 5 am the Government night rate applies.

Autos are available for trips in and around Vellore town and Katpadi. For longer trips the app offers a car.

How to book

  1. Open velloretaxi.app on your phone. Nothing to install.
  2. Choose your pickup and drop. The fare for every vehicle is shown before you book.
  3. Confirm with an OTP on your mobile number. You get the driver’s name and vehicle number when a driver accepts.

Questions

What is the auto fare in Vellore?

₹50 for the first 1.8 km, then ₹25 a km, as set by the Tamil Nadu Government. Night rate applies from 10 pm to 5 am.

Is there a waiting charge?

The Government fare includes a waiting charge of ₹1 per minute.

How many people can travel in an auto?

Three passengers.
