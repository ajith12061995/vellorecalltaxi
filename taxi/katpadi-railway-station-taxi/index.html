About this trip

Katpadi Junction is the main railway station for Vellore. Most passengers go on to CMC Hospital, VIT, Sripuram Golden Temple or the bus stands.

With Vellore Taxi the fare is fixed by distance and shown in the app before you confirm, so there is nothing to negotiate at the station.

How to book

  1. Open velloretaxi.app on your phone. Nothing to install.
  2. Choose your pickup and drop. The fare for every vehicle is shown before you book.
  3. Confirm with an OTP on your mobile number. You get the driver’s name and vehicle number when a driver accepts.

Questions

How much is a taxi from Katpadi station to CMC Hospital?

The trip is about 7 km. The table on this page shows today’s fare for each vehicle for that distance. Your exact fare is shown in the app before you book.

Can I book before my train arrives?

Yes. Choose a pickup time in the app and schedule the ride, or book when you are near Katpadi.

Do you have autos at Katpadi?

Yes. Autos run on the Tamil Nadu Government fare: ₹50 for the first 1.8 km and ₹25 for each km after that.
